Claire is your trusted gateway for everyday finance. It follows your preferences, stays within your risk guardrails, optimize your financial portfolio, and takes care of trivial financial tasks on your behalf.

## What is Claire?

Claire serves as your intelligent financial interface in the agent economy, acting as both custodian and advisor for your digital assets. Rather than requiring you to navigate complex blockchain transactions or multiple financial platforms, Claire handles the technical complexity while keeping you in control of your financial decisions.
As your personal gateway agent, Claire understands your spending patterns, risk tolerance, and financial goals. It automatically manages routine transactions, optimizes your portfolio allocation across different assets and networks, and provides expert assessment when other agents request payments on your behalf. When you interact with various financial agents throughout the ecosystem, Claire acts as your trusted intermediary, evaluating transaction legitimacy and offering personalized recommendations based on your financial profile.
