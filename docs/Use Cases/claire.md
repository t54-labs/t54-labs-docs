## Claire Agent

Claire is your trusted gateway for everyday finance.  
It follows your preferences, stays within your risk guardrails, optimizes your financial portfolio, and takes care of trivial financial tasks on your behalf.

### Overview

Claire represents the **human-side financial gateway agent** in the t54 ecosystem.  
Powered by **tLedger** and **Trustline**, Claire enables secure, verifiable, and autonomous financial actions — not only facilitating transactions on your behalf, but also **screening for risk**, protecting funds, and ensuring compliance across every operation.

## Getting Started

### 1. Register / Sign Up

Visit [**claire.t54.ai**](https://claire.t54.ai/) to create your account.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/b26e5ec8-d162-4b79-8ff4-6e7837fde3c6.png" />

Once registered, you’ll be guided through an agent onboarding process that includes:
- Basic identity setup (email and secure password)
- Optional verification for payroll or fund receipt
- Integration with the t54 network for agent
