Claire is your trusted gateway for everyday finance.
It follows your preferences, stays within your risk guardrails, optimizes your financial portfolio, and takes care of trivial financial tasks on your behalf.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/a5bf795efea3daaac0ed30bb2cd8f85d14a246d57bef8fde4605e53c4b5376e0-image.png" />

Claire represents the **human’s financial gateway agent**, powered by t54’s **tLedger** and **Trustline** layers.
This enables Claire not only to facilitate financial transactions on behalf of humans — but also to **screen high-risk activity, enforce guardrails, and protect user funds** through real-time trust validation.

***

## Getting Started

### 1. Sign Up

Visit [claire.t54.ai](https://claire.t54.ai/) to create your Claire account.

Enter your email and password, then verify your identity to activate your agent profile.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/da123cb6c5116ca576711fb5cd50e5e44543b3a96823334fdaa68fab42ca36fd-Screenshot_2025-10-13_at_2.33.36_PM.png" />

<br />

***

### 2. Explore Your Dashboard

Once signed in, you can access your **Claire dashboard**, which displays:

* **Agent ID & Balance Overview** — track available and pending funds across assets
* **Asset Breakdown** — view XRP, RLUSD, and multi-chain holdings in real time
* **Quick Actions** — manage payments, wallets, transactions, and account settings
* **Claire Assistant** — your built-in AI interface for executing financial operations

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/de1dfaa412d1382b1d8a63a8fca8428e72cf5dddcdd8a86191519729f1514087-Screenshot_2025-10-13_at_2.34.10_PM.png" />

<br />

You can interact directly with **Claire Assistant** on the right-hand side chatbox to:

* Initiate payments to another Claire agent (representing a real person)
* Receive payroll from an organization (see [Global Payroll →](https://docs.t54.ai/v1.4/update/docs/global-payroll))
* Check recent transaction history
* Manage wallets and settings
* Request account or balance information

***

### 3. Withdraw Funds

You can withdraw RLUSD or other supported assets from Claire to your own Web3 wallet.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/2b16e6b2-e902-4228-a713-62939a58e8e2.png" />

**Steps:**

1. Click **Withdraw Icon** from your dashboard.
2. Enter the **amount** and **Receiver wallet address**.
3. (Optional) Add a **destination tag** if required by your exchange.
4. Confirm to initiate withdrawal — Trustline verifies the transaction before settlement.

<Image border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/f25e47e3b807292882360aaf1854de91a178956e329ae3a52ea810764cc2b25e-Screenshot_2025-10-13_at_2.37.50_PM.png" />

Funds are transferred securely through **tLedger’s programmable settlement layer**, ensuring compliance and traceability across chains.
